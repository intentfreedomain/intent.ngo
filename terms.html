Terms

Use the infrastructure responsibly.

Using Intent services means accepting the applicable terms and acceptable-use requirements.

Account limits

Intent FreeDomain currently limits users to one account and three domains per account unless administration approves a higher limit. Domains of three characters or fewer are not available for public registration.

Prohibited activity

Do not use Intent to facilitate malware, phishing, fraud, spam, unauthorized access, attacks, copyright infringement, deceptive impersonation or attempts to circumvent security controls.

Enforcement

Intent may suspend accounts, remove domains or DNS records, block abusive identifiers and preserve relevant evidence when violations are suspected.
