Acceptable use

Keep shared infrastructure safe.

Intent services are intended for legitimate websites, applications, educational projects, experiments and communities.

Not permitted

  • Phishing, credential theft, impersonation or fraud.
  • Malware, ransomware, botnets or malicious downloads.
  • DDoS, unauthorized scanning, exploitation or attacks.
  • Spam and unsolicited bulk messaging.
  • Illegal activity or deceptive content.
  • Abuse of shared resources or attempts to bypass service limits.

Reporting abuse

If you discover a domain or service that appears to violate the policy, report it to the Intent team through the official support channels.
