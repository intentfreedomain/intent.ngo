About Intent

A non-profit building useful internet infrastructure.

Intent exists to make the web more accessible through simple, useful and reliable infrastructure.

Why Intent exists

Getting online can involve domain costs, DNS configuration, hosting requirements and technical barriers. Intent was created to simplify that path, starting with free subdomains and growing toward a connected infrastructure platform.

Who it is for

Developers, students, creators, open-source projects, communities and independent builders who need a practical way to publish and experiment online.

How we think

Infrastructure should be accessible without becoming careless. Intent balances low barriers with abuse prevention, security, reliability and responsible operation.
