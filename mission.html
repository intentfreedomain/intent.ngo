Our mission

Make the web more accessible.

Reduce the barriers between an idea and putting that idea on the internet.

Access

People should have opportunities to participate in the web regardless of whether they can afford traditional infrastructure.

Experimentation

People learn by building. Real infrastructure makes prototypes, projects and experiments more meaningful.

Openness

We support an open and accessible internet where developers and creators can publish independently.

Responsibility

Accessible infrastructure must remain secure, stable and resistant to abuse.
