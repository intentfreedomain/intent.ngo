Privacy

Privacy without selling your information.

Intent FreeDomain does not sell personal information.

Information

Depending on the service, Intent may collect account information, subdomain metadata, security and abuse-prevention logs, IP addresses, user agents, timestamps and records of actions.

Why it is used

Information is used to operate services, manage registrations and DNS, prevent abuse and security threats, investigate violations, provide support and respond to lawful requests.

Retention and disclosure

Security and investigation records may be retained as reasonably necessary. Information may be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to address abuse, fraud, security or protection of users and infrastructure.
