Security

Security is part of the infrastructure.

Intent uses access controls, monitoring, logging, service isolation, updates and abuse prevention appropriate to its services.

Responsible disclosure

Potential vulnerabilities should be reported responsibly to contact@int.yt. Avoid accessing or modifying information belonging to other users and avoid disruptive testing.

DNS and account security

Intent may investigate or restrict configurations involved in phishing, malware, fraud or other abuse. Users are responsible for protecting their own credentials.

No absolute guarantee

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Intent continuously reviews its infrastructure and practices as the project evolves.
